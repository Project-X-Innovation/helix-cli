## Description
Hey Helix, today starts Marketing Camp at the Helix team. We are focusing on making promotional videos and content for Helix and sharing them on LinkedIn, YouTube, X, and possibly TikTok.

What is Core Content?

Core content demonstrates some value of Helix in action and very clearly, in a video or other form of content, to a potential customer on LinkedIn or X. We want to do that on repeat many times and say, "Do you find this interesting?" Additional content can be humorous, can be edgy, can be sharp, can be clever, and is more kind of traditional marketing vibes than just "here is what it does" and you find it interesting.
