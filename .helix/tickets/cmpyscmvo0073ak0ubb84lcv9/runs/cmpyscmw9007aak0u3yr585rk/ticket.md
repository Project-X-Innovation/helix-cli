## Title
NetSuite's own AI Connector, aka MCP.

## Description
One of the things that we've been thinking about is actually allowing Helix to extend Helix's own MCP. It's kind of like a type of execute, right? He creates a tool, and that becomes now part of the MCP. It has its own permissioning. It has its own log, and whoever is meant to be allowed to use it can use it. Whoever is not can't. It could be Netsuite users, non-Netsuite users, even anyone in the company. It has auditing and permissioning, and it can leverage all of the Helix knowledge to accomplish whatever tasks they need. Helix can build any arbitrary tool, and that'll be part of their MCP. 



Now of course one of the foundations in the manifest of Helix and the way we do things is that we don't build things that NetSuite will build. My question is, how close does this get to something that NetSuite is going to take ownership over or is this still out of their range? Basically it's extending custom workflows to the AI, right, to their Claude AI, to their ChatGPT. Now I believe NetSuite, in their very own MCP/AI connector, allows for custom tools. One way we can do this is just to create custom tools. The question is, what kind of leverage do custom tools have? Is that just the offer we bring, that we can create the custom tools and have it automatically deployed as part of their account? You can deep dive into the NetSuite documentation and comments and all sorts of things people are seeing on the Internet and their official documents, of course, or is there room for somehow Helixing this up?
